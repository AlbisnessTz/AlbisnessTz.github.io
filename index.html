About

My parents are agriculturalists — the land taught me patience, and how something small and unpromising can be tended into something that feeds people. I bring that same mindset to engineering: start small, tend it daily, let it grow.

I taught myself C, C++, and C# from a phone before I ever touched a laptop with real resources. I now work across AI-assisted development, Android app building through mobile IDEs, and prompt engineering — using AI tools to design and ship systems end to end.

Outside of engineering, I edit and format manuscripts for independent authors, and I create content across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram — some of it technical, some of it spiritual. I lead with faith as much as I lead with logic.
